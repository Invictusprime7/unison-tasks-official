# Fix AI Builder acceptance and promote four 21st designs

## Outcome
- AI Builder edits pass the canonical commit path when they preserve the site contract, with actionable failures only for real structural violations.
- Wizard composition stops failing on catalog-role drift and remains deterministic when AI enrichment is invalid.
- Pricing Table, Floating Navbar, Product Cards, and Signal CTA are owner-authorized, certified, compilable, and selectable across every industry style.

## Implementation
1. Align the wizard composer’s advertised catalog, backend validation, and client validation around the same role-scoped variant rules; keep navbar/footer compiler-owned and prevent invalid chrome selections/copy from causing 502 repairs.
2. Trace and correct the AI Builder layout/commit handoff so diagnostic-only layout findings do not reject otherwise valid canonical edits; retain hard rejection for sealed files, syntax errors, identity failures, and broken previews.
3. Convert the four supplied/requested designs from retired to owner-authorized source adaptations, complete their compatibility/certification records, and promote them as preferred portable variants.
4. Automatically register each design in the canonical Variant Registry and every Art Direction Pack for its family; expand page-role eligibility so each can compile wherever that family is valid across industries.
5. Add fixtures covering catalog acceptance, AI commit acceptance, all-pack availability, provenance, portable recipe parity, and generated-site compilation.

## Verification

- Run focused composition, VFS commit, promotion, and renderer parity tests, then the full test suite and TypeScript checks.
- Confirm the preview build is clean and exercise a wizard composition plus an AI Builder edit in the live preview.
